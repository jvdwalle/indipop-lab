My research on harp seals aims to understand how environmental change affects their current and projected population dynamics. Harp seals depend strongly on sea ice, particularly during the breeding season, making them especially sensitive to changes in ice conditions and extreme weather events. In addition, the Northwest Atlantic population is hunted, which adds another layer of anthropogenic pressure on the population. I am interested in how past and current environmental pressures are shaping the life histories of this population and its potential to adapt. This work is conducted in collaboration with the Department of Fisheries and Oceans Canada.
